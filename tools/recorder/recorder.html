Sound Sprouts Recorder

Record each clip the way you'd say it to her. Anything you skip keeps the computer voice, and re-recording a clip replaces it. Tips:

  • Use a quiet room and keep the mic about a hand's width from your mouth.
  • For letter sounds, say only the sound. Hold s and n for about a second; keep t and p short and whispered.
  • Press Record, say the sound, then press Stop (it stops by itself after 4 seconds).
  • Listen to the take. If you like it, press Save; the saved version is trimmed, levelled, and played back.
